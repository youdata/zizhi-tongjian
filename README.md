# 资治通鉴 · Zizhi Tongjian — a zoomable timeline

https://youdata.github.io/zizhi-tongjian/

司马光《资治通鉴》全书二百九十四卷（前403—959）的可缩放时间长卷：每一条编年记事化为事件，排在它所讲的国家一行，按类着色
（政事、战争外交、制度谏议、人物灾异）；点开卡片可读关键原句与整段原文，注明卷次。人物画成生卒年（据维基数据与本书），臣光曰等史论
另成一行；时代、帝王、年号三行在顶部。默认中文，右上角可切换 English。

The whole Comprehensive Mirror for Aid in Government (Sima Guang, 1084), all 294 juan from 403 BCE to 959 CE, as a
zoomable timeline: 26,894 dated entries turned into 16,729 events in the rows of the states they are about, coloured
by kind; each card gives the key sentence and the whole passage of the original with its juan; 13,988 people drawn as
their lives where known (Wikidata and the book), Sima Guang's comments in a row of their own, and the dynasty, ruler
and reign-title rows on top. Zoom is time only. Find any event or person in English or Chinese (pinyin works too).

- Text: the Wikisource transcription of 资治通鉴 (public domain). Life dates: Wikidata (CC0).
- The events, titles and summaries were written for this timeline from the text; they can be wrong — the card's
  original passage is the authority.
- Built with rx-timeline. `data/`: `timeline.json.gz` (the rows and events), `cards/NNN.json.gz` (each juan's cards,
  fetched on the first click into it).
